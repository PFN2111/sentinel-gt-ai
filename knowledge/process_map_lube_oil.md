# Process Map — Lube Oil Temperature

## Decision flow
1. Compare lube supply temperature with ambient temperature and load.
2. Supply temperature high on every drain points at the cooler.
3. One drain stepping away from the others is a drain sensor.
4. Header pressure collapsing is a separate oil-supply problem.

## Quick checks
- Supply temperature versus ambient
- Drain temperatures as a group, then any single outlier
- Header pressure steady or falling
